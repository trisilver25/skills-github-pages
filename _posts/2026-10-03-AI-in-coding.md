---
title: "AI in coding"
date: 2026-10-03
---
AI is changing coding by automating the repetitive parts, like boilerplate, completions, debugging, and documentation, so developers can focus on design,
problem-solving, and review. The work is shifting from writing every line by hand to directing AI tools and validating what they produce, which speeds up
development but raises new concerns around code quality, security, and over-reliance.
